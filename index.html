    Learning cycle

    Learn a topic, practise it, see what is weak, revise on a schedule, and repeat until it is mastered. Everything on this page comes from the results you record. Nothing is estimated or invented.

    How statuses and review dates are decided

    Status of a topic (from the practice sessions you log for it):

    • Needs more data: fewer than 10 questions logged in total.
    • Weak: accuracy below 60% over your latest 3 sessions.
    • Developing: 60% to 79%.
    • Strong: 80% or more.
    • Mastered: Strong, with at least 2 sessions on different days, at least 15 questions in total, and 80% or more across your last two sessions (which must be on different days).

    Revision schedule: reviews come after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days. A session scoring below 60% sends the topic back to a 1-day review. A session scoring 80% or more moves it forward. A score in between repeats the current step. After the 60-day review is passed, the topic leaves the queue. Sessions with fewer than 5 questions do not change the schedule.

    Sources: results from AI-generated questions are kept separate from official past papers, because AI questions and answer keys can contain mistakes. There is no “chance of qualifying” figure, because the data cannot support one.
